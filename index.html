Approval Security and Separation of Duties

A user who initiates a financial request must not approve that same request. Approval authority must depend on assigned roles and authorization limits. High-value transactions may require additional approval levels. Every decision must record the reviewer, timestamp, decision and reason in an immutable audit trail.

Disbursement Security & Financial Controls
  • Only approved and eligible beneficiaries may receive programme support.
  • Every payment must be within the approved programme budget and beneficiary limit.
  • The person requesting a payment must not approve their own request.
  • Payment execution must be verified against the payment provider's confirmed response.
  • Duplicate requests and payments must be prevented using unique references and idempotency controls.
  • Failed payments must not be reported as successful or deducted as completed payments.
  • Every action must be recorded in an immutable audit trail.

Audit Records Export

Export functionality will be enabled after backend integration. Access to sensitive audit records will be restricted by role.

Audit Integrity & Security Policy
  • Audit records must be generated by trusted server-side processes.
  • Audit records must not be editable or deletable through the dashboard.
  • Every financial action must retain its initiating user and approval history.
  • Failed login attempts and unauthorised access attempts must be recorded.
  • Sensitive passwords, secret keys and authentication tokens must never appear in audit records.
  • Audit access and exports must themselves be recorded.
  • Audit records must be protected against unauthorised alteration or deletion.

KYC Privacy & Verification Policy
  • Collect only identity information necessary for lawful programme administration.
  • Use approved and lawful verification methods.
  • Never display full NIN, passport numbers or sensitive identity documents in ordinary tables.
  • Use role-based access and record every document access and verification decision.
  • Potential duplicate matches must be reviewed by an authorised officer; automated matching alone must not reject a beneficiary.
  • Verification must be separate from political affiliation, campaign participation and voting preference.
  • Apply appropriate retention and deletion policies to personal documents.
